About the manhwa

Author & artist: 2LYear: 202316+FantasyPsychologicalRomanceSchool

Jung Min is a loner at high school, bullied by Joo Hyun, a model of diligence and superiority. They were friends in elementary school, but it ended abruptly when Joo Hyun demanded that Jung Min speak only to her. Jung Min now finds peace only in her dreams, until an unknown young man arrives who wants to see justice prevail and grant her freedom.

The manhwa became popular in South Korea and is a favorite among many readers.
