Two worlds

Jung Min lives two lives: one in the dream and one in reality.

The dream

Jung Min’s lucid dreams are where she escapes. Here she meets Si Yun, who offers her power.

Reality

School, bullying and loneliness. Joo Hyun targets her, and only small kindnesses break through.
